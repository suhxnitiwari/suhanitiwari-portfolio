Before the interface came the system

Business rules had to become relationships.

A rider can take many rides. A driver completes many rides, but a ride has no driver until someone accepts it. A payment exists only once a ride is completed. Those rules had to become tables, keys and constraints before they could become screens.

Also built: this website. C# and ASP.NET Core MVC on .NET 10, containerized with Docker and deployed on Render. Every page you're reading runs on it.
